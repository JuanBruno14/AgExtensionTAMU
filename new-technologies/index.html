Virtual Fence Collars

GPS collars keep cattle inside a boundary you draw on a phone, with no wire. They save labor and fence, but they charge a yearly fee for every collar.

  • Step 1: pick a vendor and fill in your numbers. Step 2: open the results tab to see the answer.
  • Net per year is what collars save minus what they cost. Break-even is the highest yearly fee, or the extra calf weight, that ties it.

Towers are set at about 1 per 1,500 acres for every vendor that needs them. Coverage differs by vendor, so ask for a written quote with the tower count before you decide.

What the research says

  • Heavier calves are not proven. One 2025 study found 9% less weight gain with collars, so extra weaning weight starts at zero here.
  • Labor saved varies a lot. The example starts at 240 hours a year, about 5 hours a week. The University of Arizona Extension counted only 80. Try both.
  • Count only fence you would really build. Fence you stop maintaining saves about 2% of its build cost each year.
  • Prices change. These are published 2025–2026 list prices. Collar, tower and fee quotes depend on herd size and terrain.
