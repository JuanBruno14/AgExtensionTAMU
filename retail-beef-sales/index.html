Should you sell your steer at the sale barn, by the quarter, or by the cut? Change any number and every answer updates on the spot.

Sale Barn vs. Direct Sales

Enter your numbers and compare what each way of selling earns.

  • Start with the table of cuts: enter the price you would charge for each one, then fill in your numbers.
  • Net profit is what you earn above selling the steer at the sale barn.
  • Break-even is the lowest price that ties the sale barn. The price to ask adds the return you want.
  • Items marked Assumption are placeholders: replace them with your own numbers.
