What does a bred replacement heifer actually cost you, natural service?

Enter your weaned heifer numbers, culling and breeding schedule, grazing/feeding costs, and bull investment: the tool walks a set of weaned heifer calves through pre-breeding culling, a natural-service breeding season, and open-heifer culling, then shows the total unit cost per head, the net cost per bred heifer after cull-sale credit, the net margin, and the annualized return on investment — just like AgriLife Extension's B1a spreadsheet, but recalculating live.
