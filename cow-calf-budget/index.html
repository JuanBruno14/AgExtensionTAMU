What does your herd bull actually cost, per cow and per pound of calf?

Enter what you paid (or would pay) for a bull, his expected working life and salvage value, and your herd's breeding numbers: the tool spreads the bull's ownership cost (depreciation, interest, death loss) and operating cost (feed, health, BSE) across the cows he services, then shows the annual cost per cow, per hundredweight of calf weaned, and per calf — plus how that shifts if you pay more or less for the bull, or spread him across more or fewer cows. Matches AgriLife Extension's C2 spreadsheet (“Bull Investment — Annual Bull and Per Cow Cost Calculator”), recalculating live.
