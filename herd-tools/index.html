Quick answers for everyday herd decisions.

Five small calculators for the questions that come up every week: how much water the herd needs, how to mix hay and supplement, how much dewormer to buy and when cattle can be sold, how fast calves are gaining, and how the herd did this year. Every number comes from a public extension source or a product label, listed under each tool.

These calculators give planning estimates from published extension tables and product labels; they are not a veterinary or nutrition prescription. Check with your county Extension agent, nutritionist or veterinarian before changing a feeding or health program.
